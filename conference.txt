Problem
Generative AI can quickly produce code for signal-processing and ML tasks, but these pipelines often fail on real embedded devices due to noise, non-stationary sensor data, and hardware constraints.
The key question: How can GenAI be combined with DSP expertise to build reliable, real-world embedded pipelines?

Methodology
Compare GenAI-generated pipelines with DSP-expert pipelines.
Identify typical GenAI mistakes (filtering, aliasing, windowing, FFT/STFT/CWT parameters).
Test pipelines on resource-constrained embedded hardware (latency, memory, robustness).
Propose a hybrid workflow: GenAI for speed, DSP knowledge for correctness.

Conclusions
GenAI accelerates development but lacks understanding of signal behavior and hardware limits.
DSP expertise is essential to produce stable, efficient, and accurate pipelines.
A hybrid GenAI + DSP approach outperforms purely automated or purely manual pipelines.

Implications for Practitioners & Business
Faster prototyping and shorter time-to-market.
More reliable edge-AI products with fewer deployment failures.
Lower hardware costs and better energy efficiency.
Teams can automate routine coding and focus expertise where it matters.
