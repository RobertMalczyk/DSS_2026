## Protocol

- Test:  `cache/X_filtered_componentmatched_7th_stage_v2.npy` (v2 features, Stage 7 ComponentMatched filtered)
- Pipeline: `StandardScaler -> SVC(C=10, gamma='scale', kernel='rbf', probability=True, class_weight='balanced', random_state=42)`

- Aggregation: mean predicted probability across windows per track, argmax to track-level prediction
- Quantization: per-feature symmetric int8 of `support_vectors_` (scale = abs_max / 127). Dequantized at predict time, so libsvm decision_function and Platt sigmoid see float values close to the original.
