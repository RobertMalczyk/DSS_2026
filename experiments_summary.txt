Experiment summary (A-H) -- Training_model, DSS2026
====================================================

Dataset:  250 tracks  (jazz=84, metal=83, pop=83), 22050 Hz, ~30 s each.
Pipeline (frozen):
  - 48-dim feature vector per 3 s window @ 50% overlap (19 windows/track,
    4750 windows total): mean+std of 20 MFCCs + mean/std of spectral
    centroid / rolloff / bandwidth / zero-crossing rate.
  - Classifier: StandardScaler -> SVC(rbf, C=10, gamma='scale',
    class_weight='balanced', probability=True, random_state=42).
  - CV: StratifiedGroupKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)
    over track_id. Fold sizes: 200 train / 50 test tracks
    (3800 / 950 windows). Same folds reused across A-H, so accuracy
    differences are attributable to the audio source, not to the split.
  - Per-track prediction: argmax of mean soft-probabilities (soft-vote).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Short descriptions
----------------------------------------------------------------------

A  Clean -> clean.
   Upper bound. Same SVM + features on the cleanest possible audio;
   essentially perfect on all three genres.

B  Noisy -> noisy.
   Noise-aware training. Train and test share the same contamination,
   so the SVM learns features that survive the noise.

C  Clean -> noisy.
   Domain-shift baseline. Clean-trained SVM hit with raw noisy audio.
   Collapses into a pop dump-target; metal disappears entirely.

D  Clean -> filtered_AI.
   Test-time AI denoiser only. Accuracy moves slightly above C but the
   collapse just shifts to a different dump genre (jazz).

E  Clean -> filtered_1st_stage.
   Classical 1st-stage filter alone. Effectively a no-op for this
   classifier; sits at the C floor with metal as the new dump target.

F  Clean -> filtered_2nd_stage.
   Classical + AI second stage. The only test-time filter that does
   NOT collapse: all three per-genre accuracies are in 0.3-0.6 and
   the per-fold std doubles (0.078) -- a sign the SVM is actually
   discriminating, not guessing.

G  Clean -> filtered_adaptivems_6th_stage.
   Adaptive mean-subtraction (stage 6). Better mean accuracy than
   D/E/F but reverts to a pop dump-target with metal at 0.00.

H  Clean -> filtered_componentmatched_7th_stage.
   Component-matched stage 7 (HP25 + detected mains/switching notches
   + adaptive Wiener). Highest mean accuracy of any test-time filter,
   but still pins metal at 0.00 -- effectively a "jazz + pop" two-way
   classifier.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
One-line takeaways
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Clean -> noisy (C) collapses into a single dump genre. The collapse
  is structural, not gradual; reducing the noise level moves C by ~1 pt.
- Noise-aware training (B) is the dominant lever -- ~2.6x the recovery
  of the best test-time filter (H).
- Test-time filter ordering is stable:
  component-matched 7th  >  adaptive-MS 6th  >  2nd  >  AI  >  1st.
- F is the only filter that breaks the single-genre collapse, at the
  cost of lower mean accuracy. H wins on accuracy but still drops metal.
